# Yuliya Mogilny Landing Page - Design Ideas

## Approach 1: Warm Editorial Real Estate

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Editorial Magazine meets Residential Real Estate

**Core Principles:**
- Warmth and trust over corporate coldness
- Asymmetric editorial layouts with generous whitespace
- Photography-first storytelling
- Bilingual-friendly typographic hierarchy

**Color Philosophy:** Warm cream (#F5F0E8) backgrounds with deep forest green (#1B4332) as the primary brand color, accented by gold/amber (#C9A84C). Inspired by the Ontario countryside — earthy, grounded, aspirational. Green connects to nature, growth, and the rural properties Yuliya specializes in.

**Layout Paradigm:** Offset grid where text blocks and images overlap at the edges. Hero section uses a split layout: large portrait on the right, bold headline text on the left with a diagonal clip. Service cards use a staggered masonry approach.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin gold horizontal rule separating sections
- Circular profile photo with a green ring border
- Property cards with corner-pinned "ACTIVE" badges

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth parallax on hero image, cards lift with soft shadow on hover, form fields with animated underline focus states.

**Animation:** Fade-up on scroll for content blocks, subtle image zoom on hover, smooth section transitions.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (serif) for headings — elegant and trustworthy. Lato (sans-serif) for body text — clean and readable. Headline: 64px bold, Subheadline: 28px medium, Body: 16px regular.
</text>

## Approach 2: Modern Minimal Scandinavian

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Scandinavian Minimalism meets Canadian Residential

**Core Principles:**
- Radical whitespace as a luxury signal
- Monochromatic palette with a single strong accent
- Grid-breaking asymmetry with purposeful negative space
- Typography as the primary visual element

**Color Philosophy:** Off-white (#FAFAF8) background, near-black charcoal (#1C1C1E) text, with a single accent of sage green (#6B8F71). Minimal, sophisticated, lets the property photography breathe.

**Layout Paradigm:** Swiss-grid inspired with deliberate rule-breaking. Hero uses full-bleed text on the left, portrait photo bleeds off the right edge. Service section uses a horizontal scroll on mobile, 3-column on desktop.

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) as decorative anchors
- Hairline borders instead of filled dividers
- Monogram "YM" as a subtle watermark

**Interaction Philosophy:** Micro-interactions on every element — buttons expand, cards reveal content on hover, form labels float.

**Animation:** Staggered text reveal on hero, line-draw animations on section dividers.

**Typography System:** DM Serif Display for headings — editorial and distinctive. DM Sans for body — modern and geometric. Contrast between serif and sans creates visual tension.
</text>

## Approach 3: Warm Artisan — SELECTED

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Artisan Warmth meets Professional Real Estate

**Core Principles:**
- Handcrafted warmth with professional credibility
- Story-driven layout that feels personal, not corporate
- Nature-inspired palette reflecting Eastern Ontario's landscape
- Multilingual accessibility (EN/RU/UK friendly typography)

**Color Philosophy:** Warm stone (#F7F3EE) as base, deep slate navy (#1A2B3C) for authority and trust, vibrant forest green (#2D6A4F) as the signature accent (echoing the EXIT Realty green and Ontario nature). Warm amber (#E8A838) for CTAs and highlights. This palette feels like a cozy Ontario home — trustworthy, warm, inviting.

**Layout Paradigm:** Diagonal section dividers create dynamic flow. Hero uses a full-width background with overlapping profile photo card. Services use a 3-column card grid with icon accents. Social proof uses a horizontal testimonial strip. Contact form is centered with a warm card treatment.

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal clip-path transitions between sections
- Key/house iconography echoing the Facebook cover
- Warm paper-texture subtle background on alternating sections

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states reveal additional info, CTA buttons have a warm amber glow effect, form fields have smooth focus transitions.

**Animation:** Slide-in from left for hero text, fade-up for cards, gentle parallax on hero background.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (serif) for display headings — elegant and distinctive. Source Sans 3 (sans-serif) for body and UI — highly readable. This pairing feels both professional and personal.
</text>

## Selected Approach: Approach 3 — Artisan Warmth

The **Artisan Warmth** design philosophy was chosen because it best reflects Yuliya's personal brand: warm, trustworthy, professional, and deeply connected to the Eastern Ontario community. The diagonal section transitions create visual dynamism without feeling corporate, and the color palette directly mirrors the green/grey tones from her Facebook business page cover photo.
